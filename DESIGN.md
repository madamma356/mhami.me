# Brand & Style
The "Healing Room" aesthetic is designed to feel like a warm, luxurious, and safe physical space. It relies heavily on earth tones, deep warm darks, and soft golden glows that mimic candlelight, fairy lights (ไฟปิงปอง), and bokeh effects.

The goal is to create an environment of comfort and positive energy, stepping away from the traditional dark-mystic look and instead offering a premium, cozy "healing sanctuary" where users feel comfortable exploring their destiny and finding peace.

## Colors
- **Warm Dark Background (#1A1816):** A deep espresso brown/black that feels grounded and cozy, like a dimly lit room.
- **Gold/Champagne Primary (#D6B47C):** Used for primary actions, borders, and glowing effects. It represents luxury, warmth, and illumination.
- **Muted Earth (Secondary):** Used for secondary elements and surfaces to maintain a soft contrast.
- **Text:** Soft cream/off-white (`#F2EBE5`) to ensure readability without the harshness of pure white.

## Typography
- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Elegant, luxurious, and timeless. Used for the main English sections (WHAT, WHERE, WHEN, WHY, HOW).
- **Body & Thai Text (Prompt):** Clean, modern, and accessible. It grounds the luxury headings with approachability. Sub-headings and explanations will use this font to ensure clarity.

## Elevation, Glows & Depth
Instead of harsh drop shadows, depth is achieved through **Warm Glows (Bokeh/Candlelight)**:
- Surfaces have very subtle borders (e.g., 1px solid `#3E342B`).
- Hover states and active elements emit a soft golden glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 15px rgba(214, 180, 124, 0.4)`).
- Backgrounds may feature subtle, blurred radial gradients to simulate bokeh lights in the distance.

## Shapes
- **Soft Geometry:** Components should use gentle rounding (e.g., `rounded-xl` or `1rem`) to maintain the "soft" and "safe" feeling. No sharp, aggressive corners.
- Interactive elements are pill-shaped or softly rounded rectangles that invite interaction.
